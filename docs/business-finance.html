Burn, budget, ramp

Bootstrap money math for SNDBNK: what the box costs now, how revenue covers it, what you can spend as you grow, and the next financial moves that actually matter. Pair with the founder plan for product order.

1. Money thesis

You’re in a bootstrap posture: a real product on a single Lightsail box, Stripe-ready tiers, and your time as the main scarce resource. Cash burn can stay tiny. The trap is spending like a funded growth company before you have proof (paying creators who stay).

One sentence rule Keep fixed ops under ~$50/mo until you have paying Studio customers. Spend discretionary dollars only against a milestone (trust hygiene, Free BYO, first 5 paid, first 25 paid) — never against vibes.

Numbers below are planning ranges (USD, 2026 Lightsail list prices + typical AWS/Stripe fee shapes). Replace with your actual AWS invoice and Stripe Dashboard as soon as you have two months of data.

2. Current cost stack

Production is one Ubuntu box (Bun app + Caddy + Redis + waveform worker + SQLite + media disk) plus DNS and mail. No managed DB, no multi-region, no CDN required to ship.

Line item Typical /mo Notes
Lightsail 2 GB ~$12 List price with public IPv4; fine early if RAM holds
Lightsail 4 GB ~$24 Comfortable for Bun + Redis + ffmpeg spikes; upgrade when swap thrashes
Lightsail 8 GB ~$44 Ramp when hosted media + concurrent peaks get heavy
Snapshots / backups ~$1–5 ~$0.05/GB-month; keep a few known-good images
Route 53 hosted zone ~$0.50 Plus tiny query charges; wildcard A for *.sndbnk.com
Domain renewal ~$1–2 Amortize yearly sndbnk.com cost
SES (Mail Manager / SMTP) ~$0–5 Auth mail volume is tiny until growth; watch bounce/complaint
GitHub / misc SaaS ~$0–20 Actions minutes usually free-tier; don’t stack vanity tools
Stripe % of GMV ~2.9% + $0.30 per successful US card charge (confirm your rate)
Working “now” ops burn Assume ~$20–40/mo all-in for a healthy single-box setup (4 GB + DNS + mail + snapshots). If you’re still on 2 GB and quiet traffic, ~$15–25/mo is realistic. Your true burn is that plus whatever you choose to pay yourself — keep those separate in your head.

3. Burn rate by stage

Burn = fixed ops + discretionary spend. Don’t let discretionary become “fixed” by habit.

Stage Ops burn Discretionary cap Total target Trigger to leave stage
0 · Bootstrap $20–40 $0–50 ≤ $90 Stripe live; secrets rotated; first paying user
1 · Proof $25–50 $50–150 ≤ $200 5 paying creators (prefer Studio)
2 · Pull $40–80 $150–400 ≤ $480 25 paying; support load still solo-manageable
3 · Scale spend $80–200+ tied to CAC only if LTV > 3× CAC Paid acquisition that pays back in < 3 months
Time is the real burn If you value your evenings at even $40/hr and ship 10 hours/week, that’s ~$1,600/mo of opportunity cost. Protect that by killing low-leverage work (fantasy Label teams, premature multi-region) and shipping the Free/Studio wedge from the founder plan.

4. Unit economics

Seeded list prices (before tax). Net ≈ list − Stripe fees. Hosted storage cost is the main variable COGS on Free/Vault; BYO storage pushes COGS near zero for that user.

Plan List /mo ~Stripe fee ~Net /mo Hosted storage Notes
Free $0 — $0 5 GB Acquisition + BYO wedge; not a revenue line
Vault $5 ~$0.45 ~$4.55 30 GB Subdomain on-ramp; thin margin if all bytes are hosted
Studio $14 ~$0.71 ~$13.29 150 GB Economic center — domain + unbranded
Label $35 ~$1.32 ~$33.68 500 GB Don’t sell hard until teams UI exists
  • Yearly (~20% off) improves cash and retention; Stripe still takes a cut per charge cycle.
  • Prefer Studio over many Vaults for cash: ~3 Vaults ≈ 1 Studio in net, with more support surface.
  • BYO (SSH now; S3/R2 next) is a margin feature — heavy catalogs that stay on Free via BYO are fine; they’re not free if they eat your disk.

5. Break-even & coverage

Infra break-even is embarrassingly close. That’s the gift of the single-box architecture — use it. Don’t confuse “covers AWS” with “covers your life.”

Monthly ops target Covered by Vault (~$4.55 net) Covered by Studio (~$13.29 net)
$30 ops ~7 Vault ~3 Studio
$50 ops ~11 Vault ~4 Studio
$200 (ops + light ads) ~44 Vault ~15 Studio
$2,000 “part-time stipend” ~440 Vault ~151 Studio
Near-term win condition Get to ops + a small marketing budget paid by Studio MRR — e.g. 5 Studio (~$66 net) covers a $40 box and leaves ~$25 for experiments. That’s Stage 1 proof. Don’t model a founder salary until Stage 2 pull is real.

6. 12-month ramp

Milestones first, spend second. If a milestone slips, cut discretionary — don’t “buy” progress with ads before the Free/Studio paths feel inevitable.

1
Months 0–2 · Trust & sellable

Spend cap: ops only (+ ≤$50 one-shots). Rotate secrets, confirm Stripe bootstrap + webhook, smoke-test checkout. Goal: paid tiers not “Not available yet”; you could take money tomorrow without embarrassment.

2
Months 2–4 · Finish Free promise + Studio path

Spend cap: ≤$150/mo discretionary. Ship S3/R2 BYO; polish custom domain + unbranded. Soft launch to people you know. Goal: 1–5 paying (Studio preferred).

3
Months 4–8 · Proof loop

Spend cap: ≤$200–400/mo if MRR covers it. Small, measurable outreach (communities, creators you’d use as case studies). Goal: 5–25 paying; write down why they converted.

4
Months 8–12 · Controlled pull

Only then: upgrade box if metrics force it; tiny paid experiments with a kill switch. Goal: MRR > ops + experiments; Label/teams only if someone is asking.

Checkpoint MRR (gross) What it unlocks
First dollar any Billing path proven end-to-end
Ops covered ~$50+ Box + DNS paid by customers
Proof ~$70–150 ~5–10 Studio-equivalent; case studies
Pull ~$350+ ~25 Studio-equivalent; justified ad tests

7. Budget buckets

Split every dollar into a bucket. If it doesn’t fit, it’s a no until a milestone moves.

Bucket Bootstrap Proof What it pays for
Keep the lights on $20–40 $25–50 Lightsail, DNS, SES, snapshots
Trust / legal / hygiene one-shots as needed Secret rotation effort, domain/email reputation, accountant later
Product leverage $0 $0–50 Only tools that unblock shipping (not “nice IDE skins”)
Distribution $0 $50–150 Creator outreach, small boosts, stickers/swag only if tied to a named person
Contingency 1× monthly ops 2× monthly ops Disk emergency, accidental overage, restore drill
Cash buffer habit Keep at least 3 months of ops burn in a separate “SNDBNK ops” envelope (~$100–150 early). Don’t touch it for features or ads.

8. What not to buy yet

Temptation Why wait
Multi-region / Kubernetes / managed Postgres SQLite + one box is the strategy until it isn’t
Native mobile apps Web player is the product; apps are a second company
Big ad campaigns / agency retainers No CAC baseline; you’ll buy noise
Hiring before support pain Contract tiny tasks only when a week is blocked
Label “enterprise” sales motion Teams UI missing; overselling burns trust
Stack of analytics/CRM/growth SaaS Stripe + a spreadsheet is enough through Stage 1

9. Monthly scoreboard

Fifteen minutes on the 1st of the month. If you don’t write the numbers, the budget is fiction.

Metric Where Healthy early signal
AWS / Lightsail invoice AWS Billing Flat or stepped only when you chose an upgrade
Gross MRR / ARR Stripe Any → ops covered → proof thresholds above
Paying creators (Vault / Studio / Label) Stripe + admin Studio share rising over time
Hosted GB used (local adapter) Library quotas / disk Not sneaking up on the Lightsail disk
Discretionary spend Your card / envelope Under stage cap
Support hours Honest estimate Still fun; not a second job of firefighting
Spreadsheet columns (minimum) month, ops_burn, discretionary, gross_mrr, net_mrr, paying_vault, paying_studio, hosted_gb, notes. That’s enough.

10. Next steps (do these)

Ordered for a bootstrap founder. Check them off in order; skip nothing in block A before spending on growth.

A · Money plumbing (this week)

1
Open last AWS bill

Write down actual Lightsail + Route 53 + SES. Replace the ranges in §2 with your numbers.

2
Confirm Stripe can charge

bun run stripe:bootstrap in prod if needed; webhook https://sndbnk.com/api/stripe/webhook; buy your own Vault or Studio once as a test (refund via admin/Stripe if you want).

3
Create the ops envelope

Park 3× monthly ops (~$100–150) somewhere you won’t spend on features. Set a calendar reminder: “SNDBNK scoreboard” on the 1st.

4
Trust before traffic

Untrack .env, rotate secrets (see known-issues). Advertising into a leaky secret story is how bootstrap dreams get expensive.

B · Earn the right to spend (next 30–60 days)

5
Ship margin features, not ad spend

S3/R2 BYO + Studio custom-domain polish (founder plan §8). These raise willingness to pay without raising burn.

6
Land 5 humans

Personal outreach beats ads. Offer help migrating a catalog. Prefer Studio trials with a clear “your domain on SNDBNK” pitch.

7
Write the conversion note

For each paid user: why they paid, what almost blocked them. That note becomes your only allowed ad copy later.

C · Only after proof

8
Upgrade the box when metrics force it

OOM / swap / disk >70% sustained — not “feels slow once.” Step 2→4→8 GB.

9
Tiny paid experiment

≤$100, one channel, two-week kill date. Keep only if cost per paying Studio < ~$40 (about 3 months gross).

10
Revisit Label / salary fantasies

Teams product and founder stipend are Stage 2+ conversations. Until then, reinvest surplus into reliability and the Studio path.

If you’re overwhelmed, do only this (1) Know your real AWS number. (2) Make sure Stripe can take a Studio payment. (3) Keep monthly burn ≤ $90 until a stranger pays you. (4) Ship Free BYO + Studio polish. Everything else is optional noise.
